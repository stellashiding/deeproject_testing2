Welcome to Deeproject

Evaluate AI behavior across interactions

In this study, you will complete two activities using a multi-turn AI evaluation workflow.

1Scenario LabReview and evaluate a human–AI interaction.
2Framework BuilderCreate evaluation criteria for an AI use case in your domain.
Activity 1 · Scenario Lab

Evaluate an AI interaction

👤 User↔🤖 AI Assistant→Evaluation
  • Read all four rounds of the conversation.
  • Rate the AI responses in Rounds 3 and 4.
  • Select messages that support your ratings.
  • For ratings of 1 or 2, select or create a failure tag.
Activity 2 · Framework Builder

Build a framework for your domain

  • Describe one multi-turn AI use case.
  • Create at least two domain-specific criteria.
  • Complete the four core fields for each criterion.
  • Only one criterion is required to include three distinct rating anchors.

Your progress is saved locally. When you finish both activities, export your study record and email the downloaded file to sding84@gatech.edu.
